BAI 7 - QUAN LY NHAN SU CONG TY SON DONG A (WinForms, .NET Framework 4.7.2)

CACH CHAY
1. Giai nen file zip ra o dia (khong mo truc tiep trong file zip).
2. Mo QuanLyNhanSu.sln bang Visual Studio 2022, bam F5.
   Lan chay dau tien chuong trinh TU TAO database QuanLyNhanSu va du lieu mau
   (chay file Database\QuanLyNhanSu.sql).

NEU BAO LOI KET NOI SQL SERVER
- Mo App.config, sua Data Source cho dung may cua ban, vi du:
    (localdb)\MSSQLLocalDB     -> LocalDB (mac dinh)
    .\SQLEXPRESS               -> SQL Server Express
    .                          -> SQL Server ban day du
- Hoac mo Database\QuanLyNhanSu.sql trong SSMS, bam Execute, roi chay lai.

TAO LAI DATABASE TU DAU
- Trong SSMS: DROP DATABASE QuanLyNhanSu; roi chay lai chuong trinh.

QUY DINH NGHIEP VU CHINH
- Chi tuyen vao phong ban con "nhu cau tuyen"; moi nguoi duoc tuyen lam giam nhu cau 1.
- Quyet dinh tuyen dung ghi ro thoi gian thu viec (1-6 thang), luong thu viec, dieu khoan cua CONG TY va cua NHAN VIEN.
- Chi ky hop dong khi het thoi gian thu viec; ky xong nhan vien tro thanh chinh thuc.
- Hop dong dai han khong co ngay ket thuc; ngan han bat buoc co ngay ket thuc (toi da 36 thang).
- Chi hop dong dai han moi lap ho so BHXH (mac dinh 8%) va BHYT (mac dinh 1,5%); hai khoan nay bi tru trong bang luong.
- Quyet dinh chuc vu va quyet dinh luong chi cho nhan vien chinh thuc; ngay hieu luc phai sau quyet dinh truoc.
- Ke hoach phan cong lap theo tuan (bat dau thu Hai) do truong phong cua phong ban lap.
- Gio phu troi tinh bang 1,5 lan gio lao dong binh thuong.
- Luong thang = luong co ban x ngay cong / 26 + phu cap luong + phu cap doc hai + phu cap chuc vu + tien phu troi
  - BHXH - BHYT - tien phat tru luong. Thang co nhan vien chinh thuc chua co quyet dinh luong thi khong lap duoc.
- Khen thuong / ky luat do GIAM DOC ky (nguoi co chuc vu hien hanh la GD). Muc thuong / phat tinh cho MOI NGUOI trong doi tuong.
  Thuong ngay le, cuoi nam, luong thang 13 chi danh cho nhan vien chinh thuc.
- Phat tien thu theo 2 cach: NOP PHAT (thu tren form Ky luat) hoac TRU LUONG (tru khi lap bang luong).
